图像编辑评分论文精读：如何设计“编辑前后一致性 Reward”

目标论文：TIEdit、LMM4Edit、Human-Aligned MLLM Judges、EditScore/EditReward-Bench、BPM、AugCLIP、GIE-Bench、MotionEdit。
方法：优先读取 ar5iv 全文；MotionEdit 另有 PDF 全文抽取。本文只写已核实到的原文和对应方法理解。
核心结论：通用的图像编辑一致性 reward 不应该只用 optical flow、LPIPS 或 VLM 单项分数。更稳的设计是：图文/指令完成度 + 编辑区域修改质量 + 非编辑区域保持 + 感知质量 + 任务专用一致性 的组合。

一、论文逐篇精读摘要

论文它怎么评和一致性 reward 的关系原文依据
TIEdit / Evaluating Image Editing with LLMs 构建 TIEdit：512 source images × prompts，10 个 TIE 模型生成 5120 edited images；20 位专家给 307,200 raw ratings，形成 15,360 MOS。评分三维：perceptual quality、editing alignment、content preservation。另提出 EditProbe，用 LLM/MLLM 中间层表示做回归预测多维质量分。 它给出最清晰的三维 reward 框架：质量、文本对齐、原图保持。适合用作 reward model 标注维度或训练数据 schema。
“reliable assessment should simultaneously consider perceptual quality, alignment with textual instructions, and preservation of original image content.”
“15,360 mean opinion scores (MOSs) across three evaluation dimensions: perceptual quality, editing alignment, and content preservation.”
“lightweight regression heads are trained to predict multi-dimensional editing quality scores aligned with human judgments.”
LMM4Edit / EBench-18K 构建 EBench-18K：1080 source images、21 tasks、18K+ edited images、17 个模型、55K+ MOS、18K+ QA pairs。提出 LMM4Edit，一体化评估 perceptual quality、editing alignment、attribute preservation、task-specific QA accuracy。 相比 TIEdit，LMM4Edit 多了 task-specific QA。它说明：一致性 reward 不只要主观 MOS，还可转成可验证问答，例如“未编辑区域/属性是否保持”。
“EBench-18K includes 1,080 source images with corresponding editing prompts across 21 tasks, 18K+ edited images produced by 17 state-of-the-art TIE models, 55K+ mean opinion scores (MOSs) ... and 18K+ question-answering (QA) pairs.”
“LMM4Edit, a LMM-based metric for evaluating image Editing models from perceptual quality, editing alignment, attribute preservation, and task-specific QA accuracy in an all-in-one manner.”
Human-Aligned MLLM Judges 把图像编辑评价拆成 12 个细粒度、可解释因素，覆盖 image preservation、edit quality、instruction fidelity；用人类研究验证 MLLM judge 与人类细粒度评价一致性。 它对 reward 设计最有启发：不要只输出一个总分，而是让 judge 按可解释因子分别打分，再聚合。这样能定位到底是 over-edit、没改对、还是质量差。
“decomposes common evaluation notions into twelve fine-grained interpretable factors spanning image preservation, edit quality, and instruction fidelity.”
“traditional image editing metrics are often poor proxies for these factors, failing to distinguish over-edited or semantically imprecise outputs...”
“Human scores or preference signals can be used to train reward models or support preference-based learning...”
EditScore / EditReward-Bench 专门做 image editing reward model。EditReward-Bench 用于系统评估 reward models；EditScore 输入 Instruction, Input Image, Output Image，输出 Reasoning, Scalar Score。采用 VIEScore 思路并行评估两个维度：Semantic Consistency 和 Perceptual Quality；最终分数为几何平均。 这是最接近实际 RL reward 的论文。关键做法：reward model 不是只看输出图，而是三元组输入；输出推理和标量；用 SC×PQ 平衡“改对”和“质量”。
“Instruction-guided image editing ... adoption in image editing has been severely hindered by the lack of a high-fidelity, efficient reward signal.”
“EditScore takes (Instruction, Input Image, Output Image) as input, (Reasoning, Scalar Score) as output.”
“Semantic Consistency (SC), which evaluates the degree of instruction following and consistency, ensuring specified objects are correctly modified while unmentioned regions remain preserved.”
“Perceptual Quality (PQ), which assesses overall image quality, focusing on photorealism and the absence of artifacts. The final score is the geometric mean...”
BPM / Balancing Preservation and Modification 提出 BPM：先识别 editing-relevant regions，再两层评估：Region-Aware Judge 判断编辑区域位置/大小是否符合指令；Semantic-Aware Judge 判断编辑区域是否完成指令，以及 irrelevant regions 是否保持。内部有 S_modify 和 S_preserve。 这是解决“preservation reward 难给”的直接方案：先定位该改哪里，再分别对目标区域算修改分、非目标区域算保持分。比全图 LPIPS/CLIP 更合理。
“modify the image faithfully towards instruction while preserving irrelevant content unchanged”
“explicitly disentangling the image into editing-relevant and irrelevant regions for specific consideration.”
“Region-Aware Judge evaluates whether the position and size of the edited region align with instruction, and Semantic-Aware Judge further assesses the instruction content compliance within editing-relevant regions as well as content preservation within irrelevant regions...”
Preserve or Modify? / AugCLIP 指出现有指标存在 context-blindness：CLIPScore 偏 modification，FID/IS/LPIPS 等偏 preservation 或不关注修改质量。AugCLIP 用 MLLM 扩展 source/target 文本描述，在 CLIP 空间估计“理想编辑图”的表示，平衡 preserve/modify。 它回答“为什么不能只用 CLIP/LPIPS”：因为单一指标会偏向修改或保持。reward 需要根据具体 instruction 自适应决定 preserve 和 modify 的权重。
“existing metrics have a context-blindness problem... biasing towards either modification or preservation.”
“CLIPScore ... tends to favor modification and ignore core attributes to be preserved”
“AugCLIP ... balances preservation and modification by estimating the representation of an ideal edited image that aligns with the target text with minimum alteration on the source image.”
“FID, IS, and LPIPS measure feature distance... However, these metrics tend to focus primarily on the preservation of the source image rather than assessing the quality of the modifications.”
GIE-Bench 提出 grounded evaluation，两维：functional correctness 通过自动生成多选 VQA 判断目标改动是否完成；image content preservation 用 object-aware mask + preservation score 确保非目标区域视觉一致。保留分用 masked aligned MSE：先用 SIFT/FLANN/affine 对齐，再算非 mask 区域 MSE，归一化到 [0,1]。 非常适合落地：把“改对了吗”转成 VQA，把“别乱改”转成 mask 外一致性。它比纯 VLM 总分更可控、可解释。
“functional correctness, assessed via automatically generated multiple-choice questions that verify whether the intended change was successfully applied”
“image content preservation, which ensures that non-targeted regions of the image remain visually consistent using an object-aware masking technique and preservation scoring.”
“we compute the mean squared error between the original and edited images over unmasked regions, that is, regions that should remain unchanged, after applying geometric alignment.”
MotionEdit 针对 motion-centric editing。评估三类：MLLM generative metrics（Fidelity/Preservation/Coherence/Overall）、optical-flow MAS、pairwise win rate。训练 MotionNFT 用 optical flow 比较 input→edited 与 input→GT motion，构造 motion reward，并与 MLLM reward 50/50 组合。 它不是通用一致性 reward，而是任务专用 reward 的好例子：当编辑目标是动作/姿态/位移时，optical flow 可以作为 discriminative consistency；但普通属性/风格/增删编辑不适用。
“For discriminative evaluation, we ... comparing the optical flow ... between the input and model-edited images against the input–ground truth flow.”
“MLLM-based metrics to assess the fidelity, preservation, coherence, and overall quality of edited images.”
“our reward function must quantify the alignment between model-predicted motion and the ground-truth motion edit.”
“50% our optical flow-based Motion Reward and ... 50% MLLM reward...”

二、这些方法对“一致性 reward”设计的共同启发

推荐 reward 总体形式： R_total = w_inst * R_instruction + w_edit * R_target_edit + w_preserve * R_non_target_preservation + w_quality * R_visual_quality + w_task * R_task_specific 其中： - R_instruction：是否理解并遵循编辑指令 - R_target_edit：目标区域/目标属性是否改对 - R_non_target_preservation：非目标区域、主体身份、背景、布局是否保持 - R_visual_quality：真实感、伪影、清晰度 - R_task_specific：人脸/商品/文档/动作等任务专用一致性

1) 不要用一个“全图相似度”解决 preservation

BPM、GIE-Bench、AugCLIP 都指出，全图相似度会混淆“应该改的变化”和“不应该改的变化”。更好的做法是先区分编辑相关区域和无关区域。

  • 有 mask：直接在 mask 内算修改分，mask 外算保持分。
  • 无 mask：用 grounding / segmentation / VLM 先估计编辑区域。
  • 非目标区域可以用 aligned MSE、LPIPS、DINO feature、CLIP image feature、或 VLM judge。

2) VLM 适合做语义 judge，但最好拆维度

TIEdit、LMM4Edit、Human-Aligned MLLM Judges 都显示：VLM/MLLM 可以评，但不要只问“给总分”。最好拆成：

  • Instruction / editing alignment
  • Attribute / content preservation
  • Perceptual quality
  • Task-specific QA accuracy
  • Localization / over-edit / under-edit

3) Reward model 应该吃三元组，而不是只吃输出图

EditScore 的设计很关键：输入是 (Instruction, Input Image, Output Image)。这正好对应图像编辑 reward 的核心：既要知道用户要求，也要知道原图，才能判断编辑图是否“改对且没改坏”。

4) 通用 reward + 任务专用 reward 组合

MotionEdit 说明：任务专用 reward 可以很有效，但不通用。Optical flow 对运动编辑有效；人脸要用 ArcFace/InsightFace；文档要 OCR/layout；商品要 logo/OCR/product embedding；姿态要 pose estimator。

任务任务专用一致性 reward通用项
普通局部编辑mask 外 preservation、object-aware consistencyVLM instruction + quality
人脸编辑ArcFace/InsightFace 身份相似度VLM + LPIPS/DINO + quality
商品图编辑product embedding、OCR、logo consistencyVLM + background preservation
文档/海报编辑OCR、layout similarity、font/style consistencyVLM + visual quality
动作/姿态编辑optical flow、pose/keypoint、depth/geometryVLM + identity/scene preservation

三、一个可落地的一致性 Reward 方案

  1. 先解析指令：用 VLM/LLM 得到目标对象、目标属性、操作类型、应保持对象/属性。
  2. 定位编辑区域：有 mask 用 mask；无 mask 用 GroundingDINO/SAM/OWL-ViT 或 VLM 生成区域。
  3. 目标区域评分：用 VQA/MLLM 判断目标 edit 是否完成；必要时用 CLIP directional similarity 或 task-specific detector。
  4. 非目标区域评分：mask 外 aligned MSE/LPIPS/DINO feature；再用 VLM 检查主体/背景/身份是否保持。
  5. 图像质量评分：VLM quality judge 或 IQA 模型，惩罚 artifact、变形、模糊。
  6. 聚合方式：不要简单平均；建议用几何平均或 gating：若 instruction 完全失败，则 preservation 再高也不能高分；若 preservation 严重失败，总分上限受限。
一个更稳的聚合例子： R_sem = sqrt(R_instruction * R_target_edit) R_pres = min(R_region_preserve, R_identity_preserve, R_background_preserve) R_quality = IQA_or_VLM_quality R_total = (R_sem^0.45) * (R_pres^0.35) * (R_quality^0.20) 可选： - 若 R_instruction < 0.3，则 R_total <= 0.4 - 若 R_preserve < 0.3，则 R_total <= 0.6 - 对人脸/商品/文档/动作加入 R_task_specific

四、应该怎么借鉴这些论文

需求最值得借鉴的论文具体借鉴点
建立评价维度TIEdit、LMM4Edit质量 / 编辑对齐 / 原图保持三维；加 QA accuracy。
做可解释 VLM judgeHuman-Aligned MLLM Judges把总分拆成可解释因素，分别 prompt、分别校准。
