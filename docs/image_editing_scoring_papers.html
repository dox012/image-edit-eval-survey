一、最相关：直接做图像编辑 benchmark / metric / reward model

论文 / Benchmark类型评分/评估维度原文摘录
Evaluating Image Editing with LLMs / TIEditbenchmarkLLM evaluatorperceptual quality、instruction alignment、original content preservation；含专家 MOS。
“Evaluating text-guided image editing (TIE) methods remains a challenging problem, as reliable assessment should simultaneously consider perceptual quality, alignment with textual instructions, and preservation of original image content.”
“TIEdit consists of 512 source images paired with editing prompts across eight representative editing tasks...”
LMM4Edit / EBench-18KbenchmarkLMM metricimage quality、editing alignment、consistency with original image；MOS + QA pairs。
“current TIE models still struggle to balance image quality, editing alignment, and consistency with the original image”
“we introduce EBench-18K, the first large-scale image Editing Benchmark including 18K edited images with fine-grained human preference annotations...”
Human-Aligned MLLM Judges for Fine-Grained Image Editing EvaluationMLLM-as-judgehuman-aligned12 个细粒度因素：image preservation、edit quality、instruction fidelity。
“Evaluating image editing models remains challenging due to the coarse granularity and limited interpretability of traditional metrics...”
“decomposes common evaluation notions into twelve fine-grained interpretable factors spanning image preservation, edit quality, and instruction fidelity.”
EditScore / EditReward-Benchreward modelRL面向 RL 的编辑质量 reward model。
“Reinforcement Learning (RL) offers a promising solution, but its adoption in image editing has been severely hindered by the lack of a high-fidelity, efficient reward signal.”
“we develop EditScore, a series of reward models (7B-72B) for evaluating the quality of instruction-guided image editing.”
Edit-Compass & EditReward-Compassbenchmarkreward modeling统一评估 image editing models 和 reward models。
“reward models have become increasingly important for RL-based image editing optimization, yet existing reward model benchmarks still rely on unrealistic evaluation settings...”
“we introduce Edit-Compass and EditReward-Compass, a unified evaluation suite for image editing and reward modeling.”
ReasonEdit / RE-Rewardinterpretable evaluatorreward model解释式评估，关注 artifacts、unintended modifications、aesthetics。
“many edited results still suffer from artifacts, unintended modifications, and suboptimal aesthetics.”
“we propose RE-Reward, a multimodal large language model (MLLM)-based reward model designed to provide human-align...”
EditHF / EditHF-1Mhuman feedbackevaluation modelvisual quality、instruction alignment、attribute preservation。
“EditHF-1M, a million-scale image editing dataset with over 29M human preference pairs and 148K human mean opinion ratings, both evaluated from three dimensions, i.e., visual quality, instruction alignment, and attribute preservation.”
Multi-Reward as Condition for Instruction-based Image Editingmulti-rewarddata scoringinstruction following、detail preserving、generation quality。
“these datasets suffer from inaccurate instruction following, poor detail preserving, and generation artifacts.”
“we first design a quantitative metric system based on best-in-class LVLM ... to evaluate the generation quality from 3 perspectives, namely, instruction following, detail preserving, and generation quality.”
Balancing Preservation and Modification / BPMmetricpreservation显式拆分 editing-relevant / irrelevant regions，兼顾修改和保留。
“Instruction-based image editing ... aims to modify the image faithfully according to the instruction while preserving irrelevant content unchanged...”
“failing to comprehensively evaluate both instruction-based modification and preservation of irrelevant regions...”
Preserve or Modify? / AugCLIPmetricCLIP-based上下文感知地平衡 preservation / modification。
“text-guided image editing ... seeks the preservation of core elements in the source image while implementing modifications based on the target text.”
“existing metrics have a context-blindness problem... biasing towards either modification or preservation.”
GIE-Benchgrounded benchmarkfunctional correctness + image content preservation。
“Existing evaluation approaches often rely on image-text similarity metrics like CLIP, which lack precision.”
“evaluate text-guided image editing models in a more grounded manner, along two critical dimensions: (i) functional correctness ... and (ii) image content preservation...”
EditInspectorbenchmarkedit verificationaccuracy、artifact detection、visual quality、scene integration、common sense、change description。
“introduce EditInspector, a novel benchmark for evaluation of text-guided image edits, based on human annotations collected using an extensive template for edit verification.”
“assessing edits across various dimensions, including accuracy, artifact detection, visual quality, seamless integration with the image scene, adherence to common sense...”
What Changed? / DICEMLLM evaluatordifference detection检测原图和编辑图的 localized differences，并判断差异是否与请求相关。
“DICE ... detect localized differences between the original and the edited image and to assess their relevance to the given modification request.”
EdiVal-Agentobject-centric evaluationmulti-turnobject-centric 细粒度评估，多轮编辑，content consistency / visual quality / instruction following。
“reliable and interpretable evaluation remains a bottleneck.”
“zero-shot vision language models (VLMs), whose prompt-based assessments of instruction following, content consistency, and visual quality are often imprecise.”
HYPE-EDIT-1reliability benchmarkpass/fail10 次重试、pass@10、effective cost，VLM/human binary judging。
“a 100-task benchmark of reference-based marketing/design edits with binary pass/fail judging.”
“generate 10 independent outputs to estimate per-attempt pass rate, pass@10, expected attempts under a retry cap...”

二、综合 benchmark：覆盖任务更多，但也包含评分协议

论文 / Benchmark类型评分关注点原文摘录
MotionEditmotion editing benchmarkrewardmotion fidelity、identity / structure / physical plausibility；generative/discriminative/preference metrics。
“modifying subject actions and interactions while preserving identity, structure, and physical plausibility.”
“measures model performance with generative, discriminative, and preference-based metrics.”
WiseEditcognition/creativity benchmarkAwareness、Interpretation、Imagination；复杂知识编辑。
“existing benchmarks provide too narrow a scope for evaluation, failing to holistically assess these advanced abilities.”
“WiseEdit decomposes image editing into three cascaded steps, i.e., Awareness, Interpretation, and Imagination...”
I2I-Benchcomprehensive benchmark10 类任务、30 个 decoupled fine-grained evaluation dimensions、specialized tools + LMMs。
“Existing image editing benchmarks generally suffer from limited task scopes, insufficient evaluation dimensions...”
“comprehensive evaluation dimensions, including 30 decoupled and fine-grained evaluation dimensions with automated hybrid evaluation methods that integrate specialized tools and large multimodal models (LMMs)”
GEditBench v2human-aligned benchmarkvisual consistency：identity、structure、semantic coherence。
“standard metrics fail to adequately capture visual consistency, i.e., the preservation of identity, structure and semantic coherence between edited and original images.”
HATIEhuman-aligned benchmark多分数组合，自动化 omnidirectional evaluation pipeline。
“there is no widely-accepted standard evaluation method mainly due to the subjective nature of the task...”
“we combine multiple scores measuring various aspects of editing so as to align with human perception.”
EditValstandardized benchmark13 类 edit types；pre-trained vision-language models 自动评估 fidelity；附 human evaluation。
“introduce EditVal, a standardized benchmark for quantitatively evaluating text-guided image editing methods.”
“uses pre-trained vision-language models to assess the fidelity of generated images for each edit type.”
ImagenHubstandardized evaluation librarySemantic Consistency、Perceptual Quality，人评指南。
“This paper proposes ImagenHub, which is a one-stop library to standardize the inference and evaluation of all the conditional image generation models.”
“we design two human evaluation scores, i.e. Semantic Consistency and Perceptual Quality...”
Imagen Editor / EditBenchinpainting benchmarktext prompt faithfulness + input image consistency；human evaluation。
“A key challenge is to generate edits that are faithful to input text prompts, while consistent with input images.”
“we introduce EditBench, a systematic benchmark for text-guided image inpainting.”
PixLensdisentangled evaluationedit quality + image content / realism preservation；Object Detection + SAM。
“it is imperative to assess their capacity to execute diverse editing tasks while preserving the image content and realism.”
“PixLens provides a comprehensive evaluation of both edit quality and latent representation disent...”
GRADEreasoning benchmarkDiscipline Reasoning、Visual Consistency、Logical Readability。
“we propose a multi-dimensional evaluation protocol that jointly assesses Discipline Reasoning, Visual Consistency, and Logical Readability.”
UniREditBenchreasoning benchmark复杂 reasoning editing；不只靠 textual references。
“current generative models still struggle with handling diverse and complex image editing tasks that require implicit reasoning...”
“they only rely on textual references to evaluate the generated images, potentially leading to systematic misjudgments...”
CompBenchcomplex instruction benchmarkfine-grained instruction following、spatial/contextual reasoning；四维 instruction decoupling。
“existing instruction-guided image editing benchmarks often oversimplify task complexity and lack comprehensive, fine-grained instructions.”
“disentangles editing intents into four key dimensions: location, appearance, dynamics, and objects...”
KRIS-Benchknowledge reasoning benchmarkKnowledge Plausibility metric；知识推理编辑。
“introduce KRIS-Bench ... a diagnostic benchmark designed to assess models through a cognitively informed lens.”
“we propose a comprehensive protocol that incorporates a novel Knowledge Plausibility metric...”
Complex-Editcomplexity-controllable benchmarkVLM auto-evaluation pipeline；多方面 metrics。
“introduce a suite of metrics to assess various aspects of editing performance, along with a VLM-based auto-evaluation pipeline...”
Editor's Choice / AbstractEditabstract intent benchmarkEntity-Rubrics，abstract edits，intent vs preservation。
“introduce Entity-Rubrics, a framework that breaks down abstract edits into individual, entity-level assessments and achieves strong correlation with human judgment.”
“standard architectures struggle to balance intent and preservation...”
VDE Benchvisual document editing修改文本内容，同时保留 text style 和 background context。
“dense visual document image editing, which involves modifying textual content within images while faithfully preserving the original text style and background context.”
TextEditBenchtext-in-image editing benchmarkSemantic Expectation，semantic consistency、contextual coherence、cross-modal alignment。
“requires generating legible characters while preserving semantic, geometric, and contextual coherence.”
“Semantic Expectation (SE), which measures reasoning ability of model to maintain semantic consistency, contextual coherence, and cross-modal alignment...”

四、建议重点精读的论文

  1. TIEdit / Evaluating Image Editing with LLMs：最直接讨论三维评分：质量、文本对齐、原图保持。
  2. LMM4Edit：大规模人类 MOS + LMM metric。
  3. Human-Aligned MLLM Judges：把评价拆成 12 个可解释因素。
  4. EditScore / EditReward-Bench：最接近“reward model 怎么训练/怎么评”。
  5. BPM / Preserve or Modify? / GIE-Bench：重点看 preservation 与 modification 的平衡。
  6. MotionEdit：重点看编辑前后 motion consistency / optical-flow reward。
